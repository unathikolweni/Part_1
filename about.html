Story behind us

he story of Kolweni Travels is more than just a timeline of a transport company; it is a narrative of family legacy, religious conviction, and the struggle for safety within South Africa’s complex transport landscape. Founded in 1979, the company began as a humble airport shuttle service in Cape Town. Over four decades, it has evolved into a premier privately-owned intercity bus operator in South Africa, boasting a fleet of over 230 luxury coaches connecting major hubs like Johannesburg, Bloemfontein, and Durban. What sets Kolweni Travels apart from its competitors is its explicit Christian identity. The company integrated a "ministry on wheels" philosophy, viewing its service as a mission. Beyond mere logistics, Kolweni Travels provides passengers with family-friendly, Christ-centred entertainment and employs full-time pastors to support its staff. This cultural pillar has turned the brand into a symbol of reliability and moral standards for many travellers moving between cities like East London and the Western Cape. However, the company’s recent history has been defined by a harrowing "campaign of terror." Since 2020, Kolweni Travels has been at the forefront of a violent turf war, facing over 200 attacks—including shootings and stonings—allegedly perpetrated by rogue elements within the minibus taxi industry. The tragic murder of one of its drivers in 2022 marked a turning point, prompting the company to take aggressive legal action against the authorities for failing to protect its passengers and employees on national routes. Today, Kolweni Travels stands as a testament to resilience. Despite systemic extortion and security threats, it continues to innovate with premium services like the "Sleepliner" and the 80-seater "Megaliner." Its story is a unique blend of high-end logistics and steadfast faith, reflecting the broader challenges and triumphs of doing business in modern South Africa.
